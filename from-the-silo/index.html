Real mail from our little farm in Dahlonega. Once a month, a handwritten letter arrives in your mailbox with a piece of our family tucked inside.

Dawn, Darby, Finnley & Sawyer

Why a letter?

There's something about real mail that still feels special. Not a bill or another notification, just a little something in your mailbox from someone who was thinking about you.

Over the last year and a half, so many people have pulled into our driveway as strangers and left as friends. From the Silo is our way of sharing a little piece of the farm with you, wherever you live.

What's inside each envelope

  • From Dawn

    A handwritten letter

    Stories from the farm, the seasons, and what we're grateful for this month.

  • From Finnley, age 7

    A numbered art card

    A collectible 4×6 card of Finnley's own artwork. The first is her pumpkin, Collection No. 001.

  • From Darby

    A family recipe card

    Sometimes a longtime family favorite, sometimes one of Darby's recipes from our bakery.

  • From Sawyer

    A scripture card & video

    A verse chosen by Sawyer, with a code to scan for a video message from him, just for members.

  • Every now and then

    A little surprise

    A small seasonal something, tucked in when we can't help ourselves.

Questions

When will my first letter arrive?

Join by November 15 to receive our very first letter, Collection No. 001, which mails in late November. Starting in December, letters mail on the 15th of each month. Join by the 10th to get that month's letter; if you join after the 10th, your first letter will be the following month's.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. You can manage your membership from the link in any email from us, or call or text 208-520-5810 and we'll take care of it.

Will I keep the $10 founding price?

Yes. As long as you stay a member, your founding price stays $10 a month.

How do I watch Sawyer's video?

Scan the code on Sawyer's card with your phone's camera. Sign in with the email you joined with, and the video will play. It's just for members.

Do you mail outside the U.S.?

Yes! We'd love to reach friends far from Georgia. Choose the Faraway Friend membership, $15 a month with international postage included, and we'll mail your letter wherever you call home.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes! Tap Give a gift and choose Gift at checkout. You pay up front, and they get an email inviting them to accept and add their mailing address. Before the gift runs out, we'll ask whether they'd like to keep their letters coming.
