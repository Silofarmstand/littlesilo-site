Little Silo

Just outside Dahlonega, Little Silo is a family farmstand rooted in three generations working side by side. Together, we bake our sourdough and cookies, gather fresh eggs, grow seasonal flowers, and welcome neighbors and travelers alike at the silo door. It’s simple, homemade, and homegrown—and we’re so glad you found us.

    Our story
    
    
    
      
    Little Silo is a small, family-run farm in the North Georgia mountains. It started with a few loaves of sourdough and a patch of flowers, and grew into a stand where neighbors and travelers stop, slow down, and take home something handmade and homegrown.
    
      
    The silo may be little, but it stands for community, hard work, and sharing simple things done well.
